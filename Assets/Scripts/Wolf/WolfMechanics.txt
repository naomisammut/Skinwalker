WOLF MECHANICS
Last Updated: 26/10/2026

Summary:
Wolves detect nearby wolves and stay in groups. When a Skinwalker approaches, they run if it is too close or hide at a safe distance. 
If the Skinwalker kills a wolf, the others avoid its body and flee.

BEHAVIOURS:
Script					Responsibility
WolfController			Controls states and decides which behaviour to execute.
WolfPerception			Detects the Skinwalker, nearby wolves and 'dead' wolf.
WolfMovement			Handles fleeing, hiding, regrouping and avoiding locations.
WolfPackManager			Coordinates pack behaviour and shares threat information.

Reaction to skinwalker:
Distance to skinwalker		Reaction		Script
--------------------------------------------------
Too close					Run												WolfMovement			
Good distance away			Hide and avoid its last known location			WolfMovement			
Killed						Avoid its body and flee							WolfPackManager
(Used in all reactions: WolfController and WolfPerception)
